# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: Meder himself — writing from his phone or laptop while actively building. Must be able to compose quickly without friction.

Secondary: Recruiters and curious developers arriving from GitHub or LinkedIn. They want to understand how Meder thinks, not just what he shipped. They arrive skeptical of AI-generated portfolios and are looking for proof of genuine authorship.

## Product Purpose

A live journal that documents Meder's build process in real-time — writing the post while making the change, not after. The journal feeds the site; the site is the journal. Success looks like: a recruiter reads three posts and understands exactly how Meder thinks about tradeoffs, debugging, and design decisions. The timestamped thinking trail is the anti-AI-slop proof of work.

Inspired by the Social Network opening: Mark blogging on LiveJournal while building FaceMash simultaneously. That energy — building and narrating at the same time.

## Brand Personality

Stream-of-consciousness. Charismatic, funny, humble-but-self-aware. Lowercase. Casual. The author's voice (typos, slang, "OMD (on mai dead)") is part of the product, not a mistake to fix.

Three words: unfiltered, alive, genuine.

## Anti-references

- Polished developer portfolios (case study format, "what I learned" retrospectives written three months later)
- AI-generated dev blogs (Hashnode defaults, dev.to vanilla styling, generic Inter font + purple gradients)
- SaaS landing pages (hero metrics, glassmorphism, "Join 10,000 developers" social proof)
- The curated, edited, retrospective personal brand — anything that feels like a press release

## Design Principles

1. **Document during, not after.** The value is in the thinking that happens while building, not the polished post-mortem. The UI should feel like an active terminal, not a finished product.
2. **Personality is the feature.** The author's voice — typos, lowercase, casual register — is not a bug to sanitize. It is the proof of work.
3. **Broadcast-only by design.** No comments, no reactions, no social mechanics. This is Meder's room. Visitors can look; they can't rearrange the furniture.
4. **Scarcity over abundance.** One author. No categories. No tags. The constraint is the design — it forces every post to earn its place.
5. **The trail is the proof.** Any recruiter who reads enough posts understands how Meder thinks. That understanding is the whole point of the site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced motion: marquee, scanlines, and blinking cursors all respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. No WCAG target stated yet — aim for AA contrast on body text as a baseline.
